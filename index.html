[ What this is ]

A research-stage simulation engine, not a magic prediction machine.

Analytics tells you what happened after launch. It rarely tells you which change to try next, or why a shopper hesitated. We connect product pages, reviews and known purchase-decision research into a simulation layer that proposes testable hypotheses about buying friction — then hands the strongest ones off to real-world experiments. We do not claim to replace human consumer research, and we do not report simulated outcomes as measured conversion lift.

01 / DISCOVER

Find the friction.

Connect product pages with reviews, questions and available signals to surface evidence-linked hypotheses about what holds shoppers back.

02 / SIMULATE

Explore the alternatives.

Change one decision variable and compare how a synthetic consumer population interprets information and weighs trade-offs.

03 / VALIDATE

Let reality decide.

Take promising changes into controlled real-world tests and compare simulated rankings against observed outcomes.

[ Evidence, not magic ]

Simulation proposes. Reality decides.

We care about whether simulated decisions match what real people do — not whether synthetic output merely sounds plausible. Two early research notes, reported with their caveats:

Research noteResultCaveat
Ad copy credibility (two-sided messaging)Human survey +0.54 pts vs. simulation +0.30 pts on a 1–7 credibility scaleDirection aligned; effect sizes did not. Directional comparison only — raw responses and uncertainty were not provided in the source brief.
Product information completenessSimulated resolution of shopper questions rose 25–50 percentage points after clearer after-sales summariesInternal simulation outcome, not a measured real-world conversion lift.
