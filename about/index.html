About Marketing Simulation Lab

Marketing Simulation Lab is an early-stage research and product team building synthetic consumer behavior simulation: calibrated language-model populations that help e-commerce and product teams diagnose buying friction, compare page and message changes, and prioritize which experiments are worth running for real. We are explicit that this is a hypothesis-generation tool, not a replacement for human consumer research — simulation proposes, reality decides. Read more on what synthetic consumers are and what we've measured so far.
