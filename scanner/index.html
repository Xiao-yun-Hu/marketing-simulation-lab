01 / OUR APPROACH

Don't just see what happened.
Test what might change it.

Marketing Simulation Lab helps commerce teams identify buying friction, explore alternative experiences, and prioritize what deserves a real-world test. Simulation is the starting point for better experiments—not a substitute for measured results.

01 — DISCOVER

Find the friction.

Review product pages alongside customer feedback to surface questions, objections, and overlooked information.

02 — SIMULATE

Explore alternatives.

Run structured consumer scenarios against page variations to generate testable hypotheses and inspect decision paths.

03 — VALIDATE

Measure the real lift.

Use live experiments and observed behavior to validate recommendations and improve calibration over time.

02 / RESEARCH IN PRACTICE

Evidence first.
Claims second.
