Marketing Simulation Lab is developing synthetic consumer behavior simulation for e-commerce and product teams. Our current focus is identifying buying friction on landing and product pages and prioritizing page or message changes for real-world testing.

Who it is for

E-commerce & growth teams

Teams investigating purchase hesitation or choosing which page questions to test next.

Research & experimentation teams

Teams exploring directional hypotheses before committing to a human study or live experiment.

What a research conversation can cover

Share the business question, intended audience, product page and any available evidence such as customer reviews or questions. We can discuss how modeled decision paths might surface objections and help identify testable hypotheses. Scope and deliverables depend on the research question and would be agreed in a conversation; there is no self-serve live scanner on this website.

How the method works

Discover possible friction from available evidence, simulate the effect of a controlled information or message change, and validate promising hypotheses with real people or observed behavior. Read the method and review the early research and limitations.

Where it does not fit

Simulated outcomes are directional hypotheses, not measured conversion lift or a replacement for human research. The site does not execute a real A/B test, deliver live model output in its visual demos, or establish reliable results for niche populations without suitable grounding and validation. Positioning and offer research remain exploratory.

Discuss a research question

Tell us about your audience, page and decision. Research-stage collaboration, timing and any commercial terms can be discussed directly.
